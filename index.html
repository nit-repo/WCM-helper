WCM Brief Analyser

Deterministic analysis of AEM and Tridion work briefs

Brief

Accepts .txt, .md and .docx. Nothing leaves the browser — no upload, no network call.

Analysis

Paste a brief and hit Analyse brief. The analyser reports, in priority order:

  1. Hard blocks that stop the work starting at all
  2. Scope risks — multi-market bundling, approval SLA, known bugs
  3. Completeness score against the required fields for that work type
  4. Effort tier, components, assets, and the CMS-specific execution steps

Every classification shows the keywords that produced it, so you can check its working rather than trust it.
