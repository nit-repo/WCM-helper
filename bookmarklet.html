Bookmarklet

Send a page to WCM Helper in one click — no View Source, no copy-paste

This bookmarklet runs inside the KONE page itself, in your own already-logged-in browser tab — including preview pages behind CMS login. Nothing is fetched or sent anywhere else; it reads the page that's already loaded and opens a new tab here with it.

  1. 1

    Drag this link to your bookmarks bar:

    Send to WCM Helper

    Drag, don't click — clicking it here does nothing useful.

  2. 2

    Open the KONE page you want to compare (preview or live).

  3. 3

    Click the bookmarklet.

    A new tab opens here with the page's source already loaded into Compare — paste your brief and click Compare.

If your browser blocks the pop-up, allow pop-ups for the KONE page's site and click the bookmarklet again — it needs one new tab to hand the page across.
