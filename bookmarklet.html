Bookmarklet

Send a page to WCM Helper in one click — no View Source, no copy-paste

This bookmarklet runs inside the KONE page itself, in your own already-logged-in browser tab — including preview pages behind CMS login. Nothing is fetched or sent anywhere else; it reads the page that is already loaded and hands it straight to WCM Helper.

  1. 1

    Drag this link to your bookmarks bar:

    Send to WCM Helper

    Drag, don't click — clicking it here does nothing useful.

  2. 2

    In WCM Helper's Compare tab, paste the page URL and click Open page.

    Opening it from there is what lets the page report back into the tab you are already working in, so the brief you pasted stays put.

  3. 3

    On that page, click the bookmarklet.

    The page is sent back to WCM Helper and lands in Compare, brief intact — just click Compare. Nothing opens and nothing navigates: the capture goes into the tab you are already working in.

Step 2 is not optional. Opening the page from Compare is what gives the bookmarklet somewhere to send to — a browser gives a page no other way to reach a tab on a different site. Click it on a page opened any other way and it will tell you so rather than quietly opening a tab.
